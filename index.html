Process

Six steps, every cycle

  1. 01
    Extraction interview
    I ask you the questions that will turn into content.
  2. 02
    Content writing
    Your raw answers become posts and scripts in your voice.
  3. 03
    Content revision
    One tight review loop. Approve, adjust, or kill.
  4. 04
    Content publishing
    Scheduled and shipped on cadence, on every platform we run.
  5. 05
    Content monitoring
    I watch comments, DMs and early signal while the post is live.
  6. 06
    Report
    What ran, what worked, what we double down on next cycle. You see it all.
